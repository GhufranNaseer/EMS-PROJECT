1. 📧 EMAIL ENGINE & PROMOTIONAL CAMPAIGN SYSTEM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Promotional Mass Email Campaign System for All Past Exhibitors:

    Promotional Email Campaign (/promotional-email-campaign).
  - Designed to target ALL unique exhibitor companies (300+ past exhibitors)

  - Auto Background Cron Worker (`Cron_email.php`) for processing pending queue items.

• Automatic Event-End "Thank You" Email System:
  - Added automated "THANK_YOU_EXHIBITOR" email system dispatched to exhibitors 
    at the conclusion of an event.
